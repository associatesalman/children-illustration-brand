## Labeling

The cast (Mia and Leo the fox), the book title *Mia and the Moon Fox*, and the
page text are invented for these samples. The site labels them as studio
samples, not client projects. Replace or supplement them with approved client
work once permission is in place.

## Pinterest sourcing (blocked)

- Pin pages linked from the requirement documents (for example
  `pinterest.com/pin/867224472018098406/`) render client-side. Fetching them
  returned a truncated page with no source link, uploader, or license
  information, so no pin could be traced to an original source.
- Web search for Pinterest children's illustration references returned no
  Pinterest results, only freelancer marketplace listings.
- Pinned children's illustrations generally come from illustrators' portfolios
  with all rights reserved, which do not permit website use without a license.

No Pinterest images were downloaded or hotlinked. To use a specific
illustrator's work, obtain written permission or a license, then add the file
here with its source URL and license terms.

## Coloring Studio sheets (`src/assets/coloring/`)

Built by `scripts/build_coloring_sheets.py` (run: `python -I scripts/build_coloring_sheets.py .`). Original artwork, owned by
klutzbookpublisher: the studio's own characters (Mia and Leo the fox) and helpers from `generate_artwork.py`, composed into new
scenes in its line style. No third-party or reference-site artwork is used.

| Sheet | Level | Regions | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| `leo-the-fox` | Easy | 44 | Leo sitting in a meadow: sun, clouds, hills, two flowers, a butterfly |
| `reading-corner` | Medium | 113 | Mia reading by a window and bookcase, a framed picture, plant, rug, book stack and mug, Leo on the rug |
| `storybook-castle` | Detailed | 200 | Castle on a hill, winding path with stepping stones, river and bridge, apple trees, fence, mushrooms, flowers, Mia and Leo |

Each sheet is `sheets/<id>.json` (fillable region layer with stable ids, the line art, and eye-shine highlights, as plain element data)
plus `thumbs/<id>.svg` for the library card. The build rejects unknown elements or attributes, open region shapes, duplicate ids and
region counts outside the level's range. Visible area per region (no fully hidden shapes; at least 400 / 150 / 60 visible units² for
Easy / Medium / Detailed) was checked in the browser. Bump a sheet's `version` whenever its regions change.
